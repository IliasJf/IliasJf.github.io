About me

I’m a front-end developer in Ben Slimane, Morocco. By day I’m a Store Délégué at BIM, so I watch every day what makes a customer buy or walk out, and I bring that to every interface I build: answer people’s questions before they have to ask.

I care about the parts users never see but always feel: interfaces that are fast on a cheap phone, work with a keyboard and a screen reader, speak Arabic right to left, and logic covered by tests so it doesn’t break.
