Why I build for shops

I spend my days on a shop floor. At BIM I run the store with the manager: the team, the shelves, the stock, and the problems that come up during the day.

That is where I learned what most web developers never see. Customers decide before they walk in. They search the name, check the hours, look for a number to call. If they can't find it, they go to the shop next door.

Before BIM I freelanced for three and a half years, building websites for people and small businesses and running their social media ads. During an internship at Lear Corporation I built a web app and its database queries.

Now I put both sides together: websites that load on a cheap phone on a slow connection, and answer a customer's questions before they come in.
